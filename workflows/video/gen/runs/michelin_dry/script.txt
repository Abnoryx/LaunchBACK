A tyre company has more power over chefs than any food critic alive. Michelin sells tyres, since 1900. Problem: almost nobody owns a car. A tyre business needs movement. They had no ad budget at all. So they made a free guide. They gave it away free, for every driver: where to fill up, sleep, eat. France started driving to places it had never even heard of. The guide was free for decades. The tyres paid for it. Chefs chase the star a rubber company invented. Michelin never sold information, they built demand and collected it somewhere else. Their audience was the guide. Their money was the tyres. Most creators have a guide and no tyres. That gap is what I work on.
