# Code beats prompts for AI editorial motion

No one has publicly shown an AI-made reel that verifiably matches the whole EarnEdits "The Psychology Trick" stack: mixed sans and serif-italic kinetic type, paper or film texture, halftone 3D props, camera moves, whip or RGB-split transitions, a grade and tight SFX, all in one piece. The work that comes closest is not made with video generators. It comes from people using Claude Code (mostly Opus 5.5) to write the animation as deterministic code (HTML/Canvas, three.js/WebGL, Remotion or HyperFrames), then rendering it frame by frame. Examples include jaredcassoutt's halftone paper-collage `vox` skills, tuzhechen2005's three.js `kinetic-reel`, howseen-ai's camera, motion-blur and SFX engine, and echris6's critic-looped `motion-video-kit`. Each of them gets part of the EarnEdits look. None gets all of it, and all of them used heavy human direction plus repeated render-and-critique passes. "One prompt" was not how they were made. AI working inside After Effects (Higgsfield's AI Motion Designer, Adobe's AE AI Assistant beta, and a dozen open-source AE MCP servers) produces native, editable layers. The only hands-on studio review found that the first pass needed rework and that timing and easing improved only on a second pass. Gen-AI video models (Veo, Kling, Seedance, Runway, Midjourney) are good for paper-collage plates and props but still warp text and cannot sync to a timeline frame-accurately. This matters for the user: their WebGL engine already reproduces the original AE project at ~0.84 SSIM, which is further toward faithful AE-grade rendering than any public AI project found. The things worth copying from others are their *process* layers: reference locks, audio-as-clock timing, motion blur, SFX placement and builder-is-not-judge critique loops. Their engines are not the valuable part. One caveat applies throughout: none of the underlying research involved watching the videos. Closeness rankings below come from techniques the authors claim and from their own descriptions.

## The viral "one prompt" reels hid 9,500-character briefs

The Opus 5.5 wave (late September 2026) flooded X with "Claude made this" showreels. These were not AI video. They were a single `index.html` rendered frame by frame with Playwright and encoded with ffmpeg ([Movez](https://movez.substack.com/p/how-to-build-motion-design-studio); [pasqualepillitteri.it](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/19007/opus-5-5-motion-design-video-en)). Rexan Wong went through the viral examples because his own one-prompt attempt "looked mid", and found that the polished ones relied on references, brand assets and many iterations ([X](https://x.com/rexan_wong/status/2103707054108299437)). Secondary write-ups describe **a 9,500-character director's brief, a folder of skills and a 12-hour autonomous run** behind one clip, and **163 model calls over about 6¾ hours** behind a watercolor short ([cosine.ren](https://blog.cosine.ren/en/post/opus-5-5-motion-video-resources)). The January 2026 Remotion Agent Skills launch demo drew criticism as "bland, untasteful, stock-looking" ([rescroll.ai](https://www.rescroll.ai/blog/claude-code-remotion-motion-graphics-2026)). That fits the content of the official skills, which cover API correctness and best practices but give no guidance on design or taste ([remotion-dev/skills](https://github.com/remotion-dev/skills)).

Builders who reached premium output name the same default failure: "a dark navy background, a neon gradient, particles… everything moving linearly and all at once… crossfades as the only transition" ([kiselas](https://github.com/kiselas/every-frame-is-code)). flaviorg puts it bluntly: **"The prompt is maybe 10% of the result. The other 90% is the studio around it — the rules, the real brand, a reference, a plan locked to the beat, sound, and an honest review loop"** ([motion-studio](https://github.com/flaviorg/motion-studio)). The engine choice turns out to be secondary. Several of the strongest repos (howseen-ai, tuzhechen2005, kiselas, Moessif) skip Remotion entirely and use raw HTML/WebGL with a `seek(t)` function. That is architecturally the same thing the user's custom WebGL engine already does.

## After Effects agents build the comp, humans still fix the timing

Plenty of tools let Claude drive AE. ishu86's server has 70+ tools with spring presets and expression templates but no demo renders ([GitHub](https://github.com/ishu86/after-effects-mcp)). The most relevant design is **solomondivyananth/aftereffects-mcp**, whose `ae_render_frame` and `ae_review_motion` tools send rendered frames and motion contact sheets back to the model so it can check its own edits ([GitHub](https://github.com/solomondivyananth/aftereffects-mcp)). Its README also admits that the render tools have seen little real-world use. LobzyJay packages motion-design principles, an easing taxonomy and a critique skill for Claude/Codex ([GitHub](https://github.com/LobzyJay/motion-design-with-claude)). Adobe's AE AI Assistant (beta, Aug 2026) writes expressions, organises projects and reads whole projects, and Adobe plans MCP support for third-party agents ([Adobe HelpX](https://helpx.adobe.com/after-effects/desktop/work-with-after-effects-ai-assistant/after-effects-ai-assistant-overview.html)).

Every independent assessment agrees on the ceiling. The AI Tribe says a human still decides "that the logo comes in half a second later, that the movement is too stiff" ([theaitribe.com](https://theaitribe.com/en/mag/motion-designer-agent)). Visionlabs found that a vague 30-second brand brief "is still asking for slop", while "a 4-second logo bumper that scales from 20% to 100% with a soft overshoot" comes out publishable ([visionlabs.studio](https://www.visionlabs.studio/blog/ai-motion-design-state-of-the-art)). mndr.ai concluded that Claude is weak at direct AE keyframing and moved to code ([mndr.ai](https://mndr.ai/blog/motion-graphics-with-claude/)). No public example shows an agent building an EarnEdits-grade reel from scratch in AE. The realistic AE-plus-AI pattern is **start from a premium open AEP and have the agent retime and re-text it**. That is nexrender/Plainly-style template automation with an LLM in front ([Plainly](https://plainlyvideos.com/blog/nexrender-review)). It is technically trivial, but no one has documented doing it.

## Gen-AI video supplies plates, never the type

Practitioners agree that video models should not render kinetic type. Seedance 2.0 letters "warp or flicker between frames" ([Kittl](https://www.kittl.com/blogs/kittl-review-seedance-2-0/)). Runway's in-frame typography is "unreliable" ([aiunpacking](https://aiunpacking.com/review/runway/)). Veo is the most legible of the models but is still flat, uneditable pixels ([pxz.ai](https://pxz.ai/blog/google-veo-3-vs-kling-21-comparison)). One Higgsfield user put it this way: "think cinematography, not motion design — Higgsfield is trained on cinematic AI video, not After Effects-style motion graphics" ([X](https://x.com/DeRonin_/status/2056519103066538275)). Two gen-AI techniques do carry over. The paper-collage recipe produces halftone cut-out props that build up on a blank paper wall, with the material wording ("black-and-white halftone photographic cut-outs, colored cardstock accents") placed in the *image* prompt only ([Ori Silver](https://x.com/OriSilver/article/2078103099986657470)). Separately, Runway Gen-4.5 can export its virtual camera as JSON/FBX into AE or Blender, so crisp type and 3D props can be composited to match an AI shot ([aiunpacking](https://aiunpacking.com/review/runway/)).

## What the user should copy into the WebGL + Claude pipeline

The user's position is unusual. Every ranked creator built an *original* look from scratch. The user is *reproducing* a known AE project, has a numeric target (SSIM against the reel) and has a renderer already at 0.84. Nobody public has that ground truth, so the most valuable things to borrow are the craft layers that the leaders found move perceived quality the most. Some of those layers likely also account for part of the remaining SSIM gap; that link is an inference, not something the sources measured.

**Motion blur and temporal sampling.** howseen-ai's subframe blending and echris6's deterministic accumulation motion blur are the clearest "AE look" signals. AE renders motion blur by default on fast type and props, and a WebGL renderer without it will look crisper and more "code-made" than the reel. **Camera and transitions.** Copy howseen-ai's log-space camera keys and shared-element handoffs, and Sunwood's ban on crossfade chains. The whip and RGB-split transitions, the film-gate texture, the serif-italic/sans pairing and **a halftone shader on glTF props are claimed by no public AI project**. That is open ground: standard GLSL, but with no reference implementation to lift. **Audio as the clock.** jaredcassoutt derives every cue from VO word boundaries, and bishpls prints lyrics on the sung syllable. For new reels in the Psychology Trick style, generate the VO first and key the timeline to its timestamps. Place SFX by transient peak (howseen-ai) or synthesize them from the same timing constants as the animation (flaviorg).

**Critique loops, with SSIM as one judge among several.** flaviorg renders key frames, scores 8 criteria from 1 to 10, fixes the three worst and repeats until every score is ≥8. echris6 insists that "builder ≠ judge", with fresh critic agents looking at the actual render and keeping a ledger ([motion-video-kit](https://github.com/echris6/motion-video-kit)). mrpaidsocial's rule is "verify pixels and dB, not intentions" ([GitHub](https://github.com/mrpaidsocial/mrpaidsocial-video-editor-agent)). The user should pair per-shot SSIM with a separate Opus vision critic run on contact sheets, because SSIM rewards structural match but misses timing, easing feel and "slop" uniformity. The aiuxplayground skill defines slop as a matter of *frequency* and *uniformity*, meaning the same eases everywhere ([aiuxplayground](https://aiuxplayground.com/skills/design-motion-principles)). **Style lock for new content.** Turn the reverse-engineered Psychology Trick project into a `brand.md`/`MOTION.md` that Claude reads before animating anything (Charlie Hills' `brand-intake`). Use contact-sheet reference extraction (flaviorg, mrpaidsocial's `reel-style-clone`) so the next reels inherit the motion language without copying the content. Have Claude produce a beat-grid storyboard for approval before writing any code.

**When After Effects arrives**, run two routes side by side and do not abandon the WebGL engine. The first is an AE MCP with a render-frame feedback loop (the solomondivyananth design, or the Higgsfield connector) that drives the legitimately purchased EarnEdits AEP for re-texting and VO retiming. The second is the RapidEye hybrid: WebGL plates exported as ProRes 4444 with alpha and finished in AE. Gen-AI video belongs only in the prop and plate layer, using the collage recipe, and never in the type layer.

## Conclusion

The research shows a shift in where "premium" comes from. Nobody's AI is generating EarnEdits-grade reels. The best results come from deterministic renderers wrapped in human-written taste rules, audio-locked timing and adversarial render critique, with roughly 5 to dozens of iterations per piece. The engine is commodity; the studio around it is the moat. The user already holds the scarcest input, a faithful renderer of a premium AE project and a quantitative fidelity score. So the user is not behind the public frontier but beside it: they could be the first to publish the combination nobody has claimed (halftone-shaded 3D props, editorial serif/sans type, film texture, whip/RGB transitions, VO-locked SFX) with a measured fidelity number attached.

Two caveats should shape next steps. First, every closeness judgment here comes from creators' descriptions, not from watching the videos. The user should watch the MP4s from jaredcassoutt, tuzhechen2005, howseen-ai, echris6 and bishpls, and ideally run their own SSIM or vision critic against them. Second, Reddit, Instagram and TikTok were unsearchable, so a manual pass (r/AfterEffects for "after effects mcp", X for "Higgsfield after effects", and the createmotion.io source links) may turn up social-native creators this list misses.
